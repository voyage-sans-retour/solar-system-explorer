Venus

Quick Facts

  • Venus is the second planet from the sun
  • It is the closest planet to Earth
  • It has an orbit of 224 Earth days
  • It is named after the Roman goddess of love and beauty

Why is Venus so hot?

Venus is the hottest planet of our solar system, with a surface temperature of about 467°C. Venus has a very thick atmosphere with high pressure. This is due to the greenhouse effect. Planets absorb energy from the sun and release infrared radiation into space. Some gases get in the way and absorb that relased radiation, and then release it themselves, but not only toward space but also the ground. This warms the planet. Venus' atmosphere is mostly made up by a greenhouse gas, carbon dioxide. Venus also has a very thick atmosphere, making radiation difficult to escape.

FUN FACT: Venus is the second brightest object in the night sky, after the Moon.
