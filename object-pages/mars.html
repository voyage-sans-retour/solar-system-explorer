Mars

Quick Facts

  • Mars is the fourth planet from the sun
  • It has an orbital period of 687 Earth days
  • It is named after the Roman god of war, because of its red color
  • Mars has 2 moons, Phobos and Deimos

Life on Mars

Scientists believe that 3-4 billion years ago, Mars had Earth-like conditions, with a thicker atmosphere and oceans of liquid water. If this is true, Mars could hold a lot of insights on life's evolution. Today, Mars has only frozen water and some magnetic rocks, traces of magnetic field that used to be present.

FUN FACT: Mars gets its red color because of the iron minerals in its dirt. They rust and give the planet its color.

FUN FACT: Mars is best seen in the morning, as a red star.
