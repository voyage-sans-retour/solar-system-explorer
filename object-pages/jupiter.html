What is Jupiter's Great Red Spot?

Jupiter's atmosphere has many, many storms, but the most famous is the one known as the Great Red Spot. A gigantic storm, it is larger than Earth.

The Great Red Spot was first discovered by astronomers from the 1600s and is still raging on today. However, observations from the Hubble telescope show that it is shrinking and changing shape from an oval to a circle.
